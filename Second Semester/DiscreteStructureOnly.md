The syllabus specifically includes sets, sequences, matrices, permutations/combinations, pigeonhole principle, recurrence relations, logic and proof techniques, relations and digraphs, Euler/Hamiltonian paths, trees, minimum spanning trees, posets/lattices/Boolean algebra, and finite-state automata, languages and regular grammars.

🎯 Best Study Resources

I recommend not using one YouTube channel for the whole syllabus. Discrete Structure has several different areas, so use different resources for different units.

1. Neso Academy — Best for fundamentals

Good for:

Sets
Relations
Functions
Logic
Graph theory
Trees
Combinatorics

Search YouTube:

Neso Academy Discrete Mathematics

It is particularly useful when you want a simple conceptual explanation before solving problems.

2. Gate Smashers — Best for exam preparation

Good for:

Propositional logic
Relations
Functions
Graphs
Trees
Automata
Boolean algebra

Search:

Gate Smashers Discrete Mathematics

Use this especially when you already know the basics and want exam-oriented explanations and solved questions.

3. MIT / University-level Discrete Mathematics resources

For deeper understanding of logic, sets, relations and proofs, university-level lecture material can help. For example, one CS discrete structures course organizes material around propositional logic, predicates, proofs, sets, relations and functions.
