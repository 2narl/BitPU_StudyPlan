# Study Resources

## How to study

1. Read `Notes.md`.
2. Work through `Examples.md` or the topic-specific file.
3. Solve `Questions.md` without looking at answers.
4. Revise formulas and definitions.
5. Practice previous university questions.

## Official syllabus reference

The BIT153HS syllabus lists the seven units and their topics. Check the latest university/college copy before each examination.

## Suggested books

The syllabus references include:
- Bernard Kolman, Robert C. Busby and Sharon Ross — *Discrete Mathematical Structures*
- K. D. Joshi — *Applied Discrete Structures*
- B. P. Prashar — *Discrete Mathematics*

## Free study approach

Use university notes, textbooks, lecture videos and previous questions together. For mathematical topics, prioritize solving problems over only watching videos.
