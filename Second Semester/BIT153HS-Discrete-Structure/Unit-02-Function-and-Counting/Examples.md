# Unit 02 – Examples

## Example 1: Function

`f(x)=2x+1`

`f(3)=7`.

## Example 2: Permutation

Arrange 3 objects selected from 5:

`5P3 = 5!/(5-3)! = 5×4×3 = 60`.

## Example 3: Combination

Select 3 students from 8:

`8C3 = 8!/(3!5!) = 56`.

## Example 4: Pigeonhole

Among 13 people, at least two have birthdays in the same month because there are 12 months and 13 people.

## Example 5: Generalized pigeonhole

If 19 students are placed into 12 months:

`ceil(19/12)=2`.

So at least one month contains at least 2 students.

## Example 6: Recurrence

Given `a_1=2` and `a_n=a_(n-1)+3`:

`a_2=5`, `a_3=8`, `a_4=11`.

Thus `a_n=3n-1`.
