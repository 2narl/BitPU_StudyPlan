# Proof Techniques

## 1. Direct proof

Start with the assumptions and use definitions, laws and known results to reach the conclusion.

Pattern:
`Assumption → reasoning → conclusion`

## 2. Proof by contrapositive

To prove `p→q`, prove the logically equivalent statement:

`¬q→¬p`

## 3. Proof by contradiction

To prove a statement P:
1. Assume P is false.
2. Derive a contradiction.
3. Therefore P is true.

## 4. Proof by cases

Divide the problem into possible cases and prove the statement in every case.

## 5. Exhaustive proof

Check every possible value/case when the number of cases is finite and manageable.

## Exam tip

Always clearly write:
- Given/assumption
- Required to prove
- Logical steps
- Final conclusion
